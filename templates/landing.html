Anomaly Scanner

Most tools show you what happened. AlphaEdge shows you what's about to happen. Our scanner detects statistical anomalies in stock behavior before earnings — the same patterns institutional desks look for manually.

  • Correlation Delta — measures how a stock decouples from its sector ETF, signaling independent price discovery
  • Volume Anomaly Ratio — detects unusual accumulation or distribution relative to 60-day baseline
  • Momentum Divergence — identifies when price movement diverges from sector momentum, indicating positioning
  • Composite score 0–100 with CRITICAL / HIGH / MEDIUM / LOW signal classification
  • Automated daily email digest when high-confidence signals are detected
